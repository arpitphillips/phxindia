About

From a photography studio to an imaging company

Photographix is a well-known name on the landscape of architectural and interior design photography in India.

The studio

Built frame by frame

Starting off as a pure photography studio, PHX India has grown into an imaging services company — engaged in photography, videography, styling and digital media for professionals from the architectural, interior design and allied design fields.

The benchmarks of the practice — a unique perspective and an uncompromising attention to quality — were established by founder Sebastian Zachariah and are upheld today by a partnership that spans the country: Mumbai, Ahmedabad and the South.

We believe our job is much more than taking good exposures. It involves thought, planning, composing, styling, arranging and rearranging, adding and subtracting — until the photograph carries the same intent as the space it describes.

The work appears regularly in publications and platforms across the design world, including ArchDaily, Archello, Hospitality Snapshots and Love That Design, photographed for studios such as Studio Lagom, Spasm Design, The Grid Architects, Design Work Group and Studio Node.

A photograph of a space should carry the same intent as the space itself.
