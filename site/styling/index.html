Set & styling

Styling

Thought, planning, composing, arranging and rearranging, adding and subtracting — the quiet discipline that makes a frame feel inevitable.

Why styling

The frame is designed twice

A space is designed once by its architect — and again by the person deciding what the camera sees. Our styling practice works alongside the photography and film teams: editing a room down to its intent, dressing it back up to feel inhabited, and keeping every prop in service of the design rather than in competition with it.

The team is a collective of young, well-trained design imaging and styling specialists who work with architects and interior designers before and during the shoot — planning shot lists, sourcing props, and composing each frame on set.
